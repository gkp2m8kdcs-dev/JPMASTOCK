Features

Built for individual investors managing multi-market portfolios.

Record trades, organize watchlists, and capture the reasoning behind every position — without ever connecting to an exchange or transmitting an order.

Interactive Charts

Candlestick, line and mountain (range-band) renderers drawn from scratch with SwiftUI Canvas. Tap, pinch zoom, pan, and a crosshair overlay show OHLC and volume.

Trade Tickets

Capture quantity, price, fees, date, notes and image attachments from the camera or library. Everything is stored locally on your device.

Portfolio Analytics

Total value, cost basis, realized and unrealized P&L, a 90-day performance area chart, allocation donut, and FIFO closed-lot ledger.

Watchlists & Theses

Track target prices and write down the reasons you want to own a stock. Pin screenshots and notes to any symbol.

Activity Journal

A chronological journal with side filtering, edit, delete, and JSON / CSV export for tax reconciliation.

Privacy by Design

No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no servers. Everything lives in the app's private sandbox on your iPhone.

Spotlight

Self-drawn interactive charts.

JPMASTOCK renders candlestick, line and mountain charts using SwiftUI Canvas and Path. No third-party chart library is used.

  • Tap, pinch zoom and pan with three layered gestures.
  • 1D / 5D / 1M / 3M / 6M / 1Y / 5Y / ALL range picker.
  • Toggleable MA20, MA50 and MA100 overlays.
  • Crosshair overlay with OHLC and volume readout.
