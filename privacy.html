Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Short version. JPMASTOCK is a record-keeping and journaling tool that stores everything on your iPhone. We don't have accounts, we don't run servers for your data, and we don't track you. The only information that ever leaves your device is what you actively choose to export (JSON or CSV) or send to our support email.

1. Who we are

JPMASTOCK ("the App", "we", "us") is an iPhone application developed for individual investors who manage multi-market portfolios across US, Europe, UK, Hong Kong, and Japan. It is published under the App Store name JPMASTOCK. For any privacy-related question, contact us at chekinaekaterina093@gmail.com.

2. Information JPMASTOCK collects

JPMASTOCK is designed to collect the minimum amount of information necessary to provide its record-keeping features.

CategoryWhat is collectedWhere it lives
Trade & journal records Trades you create (quantity, price, fees, date, notes), watchlist items, theses, and journal notes. On your iPhone, inside the app's private sandbox.
Photos you attach Images you choose to attach to trades or notes (from the camera or photo library). On your iPhone, inside the app's private sandbox.
App preferences Display preferences (theme, disclaimers acknowledged, default settings). On your iPhone.
Crash diagnostics None collected by JPMASTOCK. iOS may offer system-level crash reports; these are governed by Apple's privacy policy. Apple system only.
Account & sign-in None. JPMASTOCK has no accounts and no sign-in. Not applicable.
Analytics & tracking None. JPMASTOCK does not contain analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or tracking pixels. Not applicable.

3. How we use your information

  • To display your own records, charts, allocation, and journal inside the app.
  • To save and reload the data you have entered, so it persists between launches.
  • To enable you to export your own data to JSON or CSV files that you control.
  • To respond to support requests that you voluntarily send to chekinaekaterina093@gmail.com.

We do not sell, rent, share, or trade your data. We do not use your data for advertising or for any profiling purpose.

4. iOS permissions JPMASTOCK requests

iOS will prompt you before the app accesses certain capabilities. You can decline any of these — JPMASTOCK will continue to work, but you will not be able to use the related features.

  • Camera (NSCameraUsageDescription). Used only when you tap the camera button on a trade ticket or note to take a picture to attach. JPMASTOCK does not record audio.
  • Photo Library (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription). Used only when you choose an existing image to attach to a trade or note.
  • Photo Library add (NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription). Used only if you choose to save an exported screenshot back to your Photos.

5. Network behavior

JPMASTOCK does not transmit your trade records, watchlist, notes, or photos to any server. The App does not connect to exchanges, brokers, market-data feeds, advertising networks, or analytics services. The App's NSAppTransportSecurity configuration disables arbitrary loads, and the App does not initiate background network calls.

The only outbound communications from your device originate from actions you take:

  • Tapping a mailto link to contact support — your iOS Mail client opens a message addressed to chekinaekaterina093@gmail.com.
  • Opening a link in Safari to view this Privacy Policy, the Terms of Use, or the Support page.
  • Tapping the "Restore Purchases" or "Write a Review" affordances, which route through Apple's App Store infrastructure.

6. Data export and your control

JPMASTOCK is yours to control. Inside the App you can:

  • Edit or delete any individual trade, watchlist entry, or note.
  • Export the full journal as JSON or CSV at any time (e.g. for tax reconciliation).
  • Use the in-app "Reset all data" control to permanently erase everything stored by JPMASTOCK on your device.
  • Delete the App to remove everything, including the app's sandbox.

7. Children

JPMASTOCK is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. International users

Because JPMASTOCK does not transmit your records to any server operated by us, no cross-border data transfer of your journal occurs through the App. If you contact us voluntarily by writing to chekinaekaterina093@gmail.com, the contents of your message will be processed by the email provider you use. Please review your provider's privacy policy.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App or to legal requirements. We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Because JPMASTOCK does not maintain an account or contact list for you, we cannot proactively notify you; please review this page periodically.

10. Contact

Questions, requests, or concerns about privacy? Email chekinaekaterina093@gmail.com. We aim to respond within a reasonable time.
