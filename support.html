Before you write — quick checklist

  1. Make sure you're on the latest version of JPMASTOCK from the App Store.
  2. Confirm your iPhone is running iOS 16.0 or later (Settings → General → Software Update).
  3. Try restarting the app: swipe up from the bottom and close JPMASTOCK, then reopen it.
  4. If the issue persists, write to us with as much detail as you can.

Frequently asked questions

Does JPMASTOCK connect to the internet?

No. JPMASTOCK does not transmit your trade records, watchlist, notes, or photos to any server. It does not contain analytics SDKs or advertising SDKs. The only outbound communications are actions you take (opening a mailto link, opening Safari for this Support page, or using Apple's App Store).

Where is my data stored?

Everything you enter is stored locally on your iPhone, inside the App's sandbox. If you delete the App, the data is removed. You can also use the in-app "Reset all data" control.

Can I export my journal?

Yes. Inside the Activity view, you can export your data as JSON or CSV at any time. The file is yours to keep, share, or import into another tool.

Does JPMASTOCK place trades or connect to my brokerage?

No. JPMASTOCK is a record-keeping and journaling tool only. It never connects to exchanges or brokers, never transmits orders, and never holds funds.

Does JPMASTOCK provide investment advice?

No. Nothing in the App constitutes investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any security. You are solely responsible for your own investment decisions. Please consult a licensed financial professional.

What permissions does the App request?

The App asks for camera and photo library access only when you attach an image to a trade ticket or note. You can decline and the App continues to work, but you won't be able to attach photos.
